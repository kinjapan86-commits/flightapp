Saved flights

Every flight you open is saved here. Prices update only when you search that date again or press Check now.

Live prices

Flight prices come straight from Google Flights for free, with no key and no limit. To also see what each site (Expedia, Trip.com, the airline…) charges for a flight, and real hotel prices, add a free SerpApi key: sign up at serpapi.com, copy the key from your dashboard and paste it here. The free plan covers about 250 searches a month.

What a search costs

Free, always: the list of flights for a date. Nothing is looked up unless you search.

With a SerpApi key: cheapest-dates calendar and hotels cost one search each. Opening a flight to compare sites costs two (three for a round trip). "Compare all sites now" on a saved flight costs two or three. Repeat searches within six hours are free. Without a key the calendar is built from free Google Flights lookups (slower) and hotel prices are examples.

Bag fees

Used only when a site doesn't say what a checked bag costs. Amount is for one bag, one person, one direction.
